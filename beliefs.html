02

Beliefs

A mix of beliefs, collected along the way.

  • 1

    Building is an art. The best companies come from love and fullness, not from fear or a need to prove.

  • 2

    Products are wrappers. What sits underneath is economic activity, incentives and human behavior.

  • 3

    Value is moving from seat to outcome. For two decades, software monetized productivity. The next decade monetizes replacement: AI that doesn’t assist work, but does it.

  • 4

    Agents are a new generation. They arrive with their own needs, tools and economic freedoms.

  • 5

    The point is human agency. Whatever I build should leave people with more of it.
